Third-party material in DawnTools
=================================

DawnTools itself is GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE). What it holds that others made:

Data/CraftSkill.lua
-------------------
What:     for every item a profession makes: the profession and the skill band of its recipe (learned at,
          yellow, green, grey). Item, profession and skill numbers, generated by tools/craftskill.js.
          Also every recipe with its materials, what it makes and how many (DT.Recipes, same file).
From:     SkillUp Forever v1.10.1, by cjber, https://github.com/cjber/skillup-forever
          (its Data/Recipes.lua, Data/Thresholds.lua and Data/Trainer.lua).
Licence:  GPL-3.0-or-later (its .toc "X-License" and its LICENSE file). The text of the GPL version 3 is in
          this addon's LICENSE file.
          Also, in the same file: what vendors ask for the reagents they sell (its Data/Vendor.lua).
Upstream: SkillUp Forever states its own sources as WoW: Forever client tables via wago.tools,
          CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db, and for which reagents
          vendors sell LibPeriodicTable-3.1 (LGPL-2.1), https://github.com/doadin/libperiodictable-3-1

Libs/
-----
Libraries, unchanged, each with its own licence file or header in its folder.

Third-party material in DawnTools
=================================

DawnTools itself is GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE). What it holds that others made, and what its game database
holds: from 0.45.0 the first three entries below are not in this download. They are rows of the database
DawnTools keeps on each player's machine (DawnToolsDB.db), written there on a machine that has the source, and
passed between the addons of guild members who agreed to share the game database (Core/DBSync.lua). A row
passed on is a copy handed on: what each licence asks applies to it as it would to a file, which is why they
are listed here, in the addon every such member has.

Recipes, skill bands and vendor prices (rows "recipe:", "vprice:" of the game database, source "skillup")
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What:     for every recipe: its profession, the skill band (learned at, yellow, green, grey), the recipe item
          that teaches it, what it makes and how many, and its materials. Item, profession and skill numbers,
          generated by a development script (not in this download) into a data file that is not in this
          download either, and written into the database by Core/DBSeed.lua.
From:     SkillUp Forever v1.14.5, by cjber, https://github.com/cjber/skillup-forever
          (its Data/Recipes.lua, Data/Thresholds.lua and Data/Trainer.lua).
Licence:  GPL-3.0-or-later (its .toc "X-License" and its LICENSE file). The text of the GPL version 3 is in
          this addon's LICENSE file.
          Also, in the same file: what vendors ask for the reagents they sell (its Data/Vendor.lua).
Upstream: SkillUp Forever states its own sources as WoW: Forever client tables via wago.tools,
          CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db, and for which reagents
          vendors sell LibPeriodicTable-3.1 (LGPL-2.1), https://github.com/doadin/libperiodictable-3-1

Dungeons, bosses and drops (rows "boss:" of the game database, source "oneforall")
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What:     every dungeon with its level range, its bosses in order (name, NPC number, rare or not) and what
          each drops (item numbers, drop chances), generated by a development script into a data file that
          is not in this download, and written into the database by Core/DBSeed.lua, or read at runtime
          from an installed OneForAll. Not taken:
          descriptions, notes, quests, maps.
From:     OneForAll 6.2.11, by Smoergi (its Data/Dungeons/*.lua and Data/DungeonDropChance.lua).
Licence:  GPL-3.0-or-later (its .toc "X-License" and its LICENSE.txt). The text of the GPL version 3 is in
          this addon's LICENSE file.
Upstream: OneForAll states its own sources in its DUNGEON_DATA_SOURCES.txt: the Forever client's tables
          (wago.tools), QuestieDB, and loot tables gathered from Wowhead and fan sites.

BiS stat weights: NOT in this download, and never sent between players. Without them the BiS tab does not show.

NPCs, items, quests and objects (rows "npc:", "item:", "quest:", "object:", "zone:", "enum:", source "questie")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What:     for every NPC, item, quest and object an installed QuestieDB knows: every field QuestieDB has of
          it (the list is DB.FIELDS in Core/DB.lua), from 0.45.1 on whether a tab of DawnTools uses it or
          not, in English, cut short where it is long. An NPC: name, levels, rank, zone, side, faction, title,
          flags, up to 8 spawn points, up to 16 steps of the paths it walks, the quests it gives and takes
          (up to 40 each). An item: name, levels, kind, flags, food and ammo type, who drops, sells and
          rewards it and the quests it has to do with (up to 40 each), the quest it starts, the spell it
          teaches. A quest: name, levels, zone, races, classes, flags, what comes before, after and beside
          it, skill, spell and reputation asked, who gives and takes it, what has to be killed or brought,
          what it asks in words (up to 3 lines of 300 letters), the experience and reputation it gives. An
          object: name, zone, faction, spawn points, paths, the quests it gives and takes. Also which map an
          area is drawn on, and QuestieDB's tables of NPC flags and race bits. Not taken: translations.
          Written by Core/DBImport.lua into the player's own saved file and, where the player agreed to share
          the game database, sent to guild members' addons. (To 0.44.0: names, levels, zones and one spawn
          point only.)
From:     QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/questiedb), whose data comes from Questie.
Licence:  QuestieDB's LICENSE.md: content imported from Questie is under Questie's licensing; the Questie team
          "is alright with others forking the repository or copying code from it under the GPLv3 terms, but we
          can not promise all contributors are", and asks to treat the rest as all rights reserved when in doubt.
          Taken on that statement, by the owner's decision of 2026-11-19; OneForAll (GPL-3.0-or-later) uses
          QuestieDB data on the same footing. To be removed if a contributor objects.
